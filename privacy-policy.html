最后更新：2026 年 10 月 27 日

新手村（下称“本应用”）是一个两关的短篇文字冒险游戏。

我们不收集任何数据

  • 无需注册或登录，本应用不要求任何账号
  • 不收集、不存储、不传输、不共享任何个人信息
  • 不包含分析、统计或崩溃上报组件
  • 不包含广告，不进行任何形式的追踪
  • 本应用仅在您点按「技能 · 重启世界」时，通过 HTTPS 向本应用自有的地址请求一份更新后的关卡内容；该请求不包含任何个人信息、设备标识或用户数据
  • 下载的内容在读取前会经过 SHA-256 完整性校验，校验不通过的内容会被丢弃
  • 下载内容仅保存在应用自身的沙盒缓存目录内，不对外共享
  • 除上述一次请求外，本应用不发起任何其它网络连接

设备权限

本应用不申请相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、相册等任何敏感权限。

儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何数据，因此不涉及儿童个人信息的收集与处理。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

新手村 ("the app") is a two-level short text adventure game.

We collect no data

  • No account or sign-in is required
  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information
  • No analytics, statistics, or crash-reporting components are included
  • No advertising, and no tracking of any kind
  • The app makes a network request only when you tap "Skill · Restart the World": an HTTPS fetch of updated level content from an address we own. That request carries no personal information, device identifiers, or user data
  • Downloaded content is SHA-256 verified before it is read; content that fails verification is discarded
  • Downloaded content is stored only inside the app's own sandbox cache and is never shared
  • Other than that single request, the app opens no network connections

Device permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data and therefore processes no children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
