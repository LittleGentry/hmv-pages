新手村是一个两关的短篇文字冒险游戏。

怎么玩

  • 第一关：点「攻击」或「防御」，把村口的猪精打倒
  • 第二关：山道上有超级猪精，界面会多出第三个选择「技能 · 重启世界」
  • 想从头来过：点右上角「重新游戏」

常见问题

点「技能 · 重启世界」需要联网吗？
需要。这个动作会去取一份更新后的关卡内容；如果网络不可用，这一步不会有任何变化，故事会照常往回走。

「重新游戏」是做什么的？
回到刚安装好时的状态，并把已经生效的那次变化撤掉。

应用会收集我的信息吗？
不会。详见隐私政策。

新手村 is a two-level short text adventure game.

How to play

  • Level 1: tap Attack or Defend until the boar blocking the village road falls
  • Level 2: a much bigger boar waits on the mountain path, and a third choice appears — Skill · Restart the World
  • To start over: tap 重新游戏 in the top-right corner

FAQ

Does Skill · Restart the World need a network connection?
Yes. That action fetches updated level content. If the network is unavailable, nothing changes and the story simply carries on.

What does 重新游戏 do?
It returns the app to the state it had on a fresh install and undoes any change that was applied.

Does the app collect my information?
No. See the privacy policy.
